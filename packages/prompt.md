# The Feed — package prompt

This is the living spec for each weekly package. It is editable from the app
(Settings → Package prompt) and synced back here. Each new package is
generated from this file, `tools/data/config.json`, and the newest file in
`feedback/`.

## Goals
- Replace 3–5 hours/day of doomscrolling with a feed that is curious, weird and kind.
- One package lasts a full week (target: 5 h/day × 7 days) with **no repeated posts**,
  and nothing repeated from earlier packages.
- Mostly offline. Only videos (and, optionally, images) need the network.
- Keep AI-generated images and text to a minimum unless a category asks for it.
- Use favorites, saves and more/less marks from `feedback/` to steer the next package.

## Categories and share of the feed

### Physics games — 20%
Simple 2D physics games: bouncing objects, merging items, breaking blocks,
side-scrolling dash mechanics and other classic casual styles. Each card runs on
auto-pilot when it appears and becomes playable when tapped.

### Condensed Wikipedia — 30%
Articles condensed to 5 sentences or fewer. Each card shows a one-sentence hook;
tapping it expands the summary and links to the full article. Topics:
- **Music history**: all genres, especially underground trip-hop, punk, avant-garde,
  electronic, hip-hop, heavy metal and transgressive experimental.
- **Literature**: all genres, especially sci-fi, sex and erotica, Beat Generation authors,
  esoteric and occult, experimental, offbeat non-fiction, poetry and alternative science.
- **Science**: especially biology, sociology, psychology, technology and mathematics.
- **History**: especially revolutionary periods, indigenous history and art history.
- **Transgressive art**: especially queer artists and artists imprisoned or persecuted for their work.
- **Unsolved mysteries**: especially esoteric and supernatural mysteries.
- **Occult and religious customs**: indigenous spirituality, Gnosticism and cult activities.

### YouTube videos — 20%
Mostly short videos and reels. They autoplay muted and have an unmute button. Topics:
cute animals, retro gaming, vintage sci-fi and fantasy films and shows, plus every
Wikipedia topic above.

### Memes — 10%
Humorous images juxtaposed with snippets from the Wikipedia section, plus quotes
from Robert Anton Wilson, William S. Burroughs, Tom Robbins and other offbeat authors.
Also memes collected from knowyourmeme.com.

### Made-up products — 5%
Weird, absurd products that don't exist and serve surreal purposes, with 80s/90s-style
promo graphics and absurdist product descriptions.

### Psychedelic visuals — 5%
Fractals, tunnels, fading colors and other generative, math-based imagery, each with a
short quote from the sections above.

### Self-care reminders — 5%
Prompts to make art and music, go for walks, work out, breathe, meditate and so on.

### From around the web — 5%
Carefully picked posts and articles from Substack, Hacker News and occasionally Reddit,
chosen to match the topics above.
